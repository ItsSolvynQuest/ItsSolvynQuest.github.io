CHAPTER 1: Late. Phir Se.

Pehla Half

Tokyo mein subah ke 7:42 hue the, aur Meguro ke ek chauthe floor ke apartment mein ek phone teesri baar alarm bajaa raha tha.

Ryan Morgan ne bina aankhein khole haath badhaya, phone ko snooze pe zor se maara, aur wapas takiye mein ghus gaya. Chaar second baad uski aankhein khud khul gayi.

"Nahi. Nahi nahi nahi nahi."

Phone pe time dekha. 7:43.

"Arre yaar!"

Wo bistar se aise uchhla jaise chadar ke neeche kisi ne bijli chhod di ho. Raat ko do baje tak wo apne game ke ek bug se ladta raha tha. Bug se jeet gaya tha, par neend se haar gaya tha.

Table pe noodles ka khaali cup pada tha, uske upar ek chopstick balance ho raha tha, jaise raat ke gunah ka sabut.

Wo bathroom mein bhaaga, brush muh mein, ek haath se shirt dhundhta hua.

"Ryan, tu har Friday ko late kyun hota hai? Koi shraap hai kya?"

Aaine ne kuch nahi bola. Aaine ki aadat hai.

Meguro mein subah ka shor dheere dheere nahi, ek saath uthta hai. Station ke paas ka konbini khul chuka tha. Ek uncle vending machine se garam coffee nikaal rahe the. Do school ki ladkiyaan hansti hui nikal gayi.

Teen karod log, aur har kisi ko lagta hai ki uska din sabse zaroori hai. Aur unme se kisi ko nahi pata tha ki jis aasman ko wo roz bina dekhe guzar jaate hain, uske upar kuch aur bhi hai.

Ryan seedhiyaan do-do karke utra, jacket aadhi pehni, aadhi haath mein.

"8:02 wali pakad li toh bach gaya. 8:09 wali pakadi toh Sakamoto-san ka lecture. Aur 8:15 wali pakadi toh meri ma ka naam..." Wo ruk gaya. "Meri ma ka naam kyun aayega yaar. Wo bechari toh ghar baithi hai."

Paas se guzri ek aunty ne use ajeeb nazron se dekha. Ryan ne haath hilaya. "Sorry, subah subah dimaag chal nahi raha."

Tokyo mein log ek doosre ke pagalpan ko bina bole maaf kar dete hain. Ye iski sabse achchi baat hai.

Train mein bheed itni thi ki Ryan ek mote aadmi ke bag aur ek ladki ke headphone ke beech phansa khada tha. Ek haath ring pe, doosra phone pe, jahan raat ke stream ki chat abhi tak chal rahi thi.

Ryan hans pada. Bahar Shibuya ka chauraha bheed se bhara tha. Hazaaron log, sabke apne dukh, apni khushiyaan. Aasman ab bhi bhoora tha, aur kahin door, kisi ko nazar na aane wali ek halki hawa chal padi thi. Ryan ne use mehsoos nahi kiya.
