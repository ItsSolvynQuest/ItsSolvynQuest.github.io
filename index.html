Story kya hai?

Ye ek computer game ya VR nahi hai. Socho ki kisi ne ek asli, physical Tokyo bana diya hai, ek giant building ke andar. Sadkein, train, nadiyaan, pahaad, ghar, sab kuch asli. Bas poora ek bahut bade dibbe ke andar band hai.

Is shehar mein 3 crore log rehte hain. Wo robot ya NPC nahi hain. Unki apni soch, apni feelings, apne dost, parivaar, job aur dukh hain. Unhe poora yakeen hai ki ye hi asli duniya hai.

Ise kisne banaya? Do dost: Alexander Hayes (25, shaant, sarcastic, 24x7 coffee) aur Daniel Reed (24, pagal, creative, 47 tabs khule dimaag mein, snacks pe zinda).

Bahar se wo normal ladke hain. Par shehar ke andar log unhe "Do Anant Devta" maan ke pooja karte hain. Wo kisi bhi insaan ya poore shehar ko dekh sakte hain aur mausam ya imaarat badal sakte hain, par andar kabhi nahi jaate. Aur koi bhi badlaav jaadu ki tarah nahi hota: baarish aani hai toh pehle baadal bante hain, hawa chalti hai, phir boondein girti hain.

Hero kaun hai? Ryan Morgan, 24 saal, Tokyo ka software engineer aur game developer, games stream bhi karta hai. Akela rehta hai, late uthta hai, boss se darta hai aur dil mein gaali deta hai. Wo koi "chosen one" nahi hai, bas 3 crore logon mein se ek aam insaan. Humein uski zindagi dikhayi jaati hai.

Story ka mood: slice of life, comedy, dosti, thoda romance, thoda mystery. Zyadatar roz ki chhoti chhoti baatein, par beech beech mein dark side bhi: shehar mein crime aur maut bhi hoti hai, aur dono creators bina dakhal diye usse kam rakhne ki koshish karte hain. Aur aage kuch bade twists aane wale hain.
