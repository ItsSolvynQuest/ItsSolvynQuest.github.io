CHAPTER 2: Kuch Thanda Sa Aa Raha Hai

Pehla Half

Raat ke 3:40 ho rahe the. Observation room mein Alexander ke console pe naksha chal raha tha. Doosri taraf Daniel ek bade screen pe kuch dekh raha tha, aur uski hansi poore kamre mein goonj rahi thi.

“BHAI! Ruk, ruk, ruk. Ye dekh!”

“Nahi. Mujhe pata hai tu kya dekh raha hai. Pichhle do ghante se wahi dekh raha hai.”

Screen pe Ryan ka kal raat wala stream chal raha tha. Neeche likha tha: 2:04:17.

“‘Zindagi mein gaddhe hote hain!’ Alex, ye banda philosopher hai! Isko Nobel do!”

“Isko neend do.”

“Ab tu bhi mazaak karne laga? Ye toh progress hai.”

“Tu isko kab se dekh raha hai?”

“Mai? Bas ek do baar. YouTube ne recommend kiya tha.”

“Hamara system algorithm nahi chalata, Daniel. Hum chalate hain.”

“...Toh maine khud hi click kiya tha?”

“Tune khud hi click kiya tha.”

“Ohh. Mai bewakoof hu.”

“Bilkul.”

“Par wo jo bug ka moment hai na, Ryan ne mic pe haath rakhke bola ‘editors censor kar dena’. Kaun se editors? Uska toh koi editor hi nahi hai!”

“Tujhe ye pasand aata hai.”

“Bahut. Ye banda main character energy hai, par ghar pe akela, aur khud ko hi roast kar raha hai.”

Screen pe Ryan kursi se uchhal raha tha. Alexander ke munh ke kone pe halki si muskaan aa gayi, aur wo use chhupa nahi paaya.

“TU HASA!”

“Nahi.”

“Mai ne dekha! Alex Hayes ne ek stranger ka video dekh ke muskurana kiya!”

“Mai coffee ke baare mein soch raha tha.”

“Coffee ke baare mein sochke koi nahi muskurata, pagal.”

“Tu bhi pagal hai.”

“Ye hui na dosti!”

Pichhle teen ghante ka kaam ab dikhne laga tha. Uttar se thandi hawa ki ek lambi, patli dhaara Tokyo ki taraf badh rahi thi, aur uske aage halke, pale baadal ban rahe the.

“Abhi ye sirf pehla step hai. Temperature ek degree gira hai. Kal shaam tak Tokyo ko lagega ki ‘aaj thoda zyada thand hai’. Kal raat tak baadal ghane ho jayenge. Phir parsu subah...”

“Baraf.”

“Agar sab theek raha toh.”

Daniel ne chips ka packet ek taraf rakh diya, jo uske liye bahut badi baat thi. “Chal, dikha. Neeche shehar mein.”

Alexander ne ek button dabaya. Pehle poora Japan dikha, phir Kanto, phir shehar ki roshniyaan ek ek karke bade chamakte tukdon mein badal gayi.

Simulated Tokyo ke aasman mein baadalon ka kaarvan ab saaf dikh raha tha. Bahut ooncha, bahut patla, bahut chupchaap. Saitama ke upar ek patli parat guzri. Chiba ke tat pe wahi fisherman, jo pichhle hafte jaal sambhal raha tha, naav baandhke aasman ki taraf dekha aur khud se bola, “Aaj hawa kuch alag hai.”

Shinjuku ke ek bar ke baahar do saath kaam karne wale log, jo raat bhar peeke ghar ka rasta dhundh rahe the, thithurke ruk gaye.

“Yaar, thand lag rahi hai.”

“Tu toh peeke garam hona chahiye.”

“Tu pagal hai.”

“Tu mera bhai hai.”

Wo ek doosre ke kandhe pe jhoolte hue taxi stand ki taraf chal diye.

Setagaya mein wahi chhoti ladki neend mein chadar kheench kar khud ko lapet rahi thi. Mandir ke saamne rakhi do chhoti murtiyon pe abhi bhi diyon ki halki khushboo thi.

“Alex,” Daniel ne dheere se kaha. “Ye sab sach mein hamari wajah se ho raha hai?”

“Haan.”

“Aur wo log?”

“Wo ise ‘mausam’ bolte hain.”

“Mujhe pehle kabhi aise nahi laga. Jaise hum koi bada kaam kar rahe hain. Mai toh bas idea de raha tha.”

“Tu sach mein bomb deta hai.”

“Aur tu usse sundar bana deta hai.”

Doosra Half

Saturday subah 11:20 baje Ryan ki aankh khuli, aur sabse pehle use samajh aaya ki uska pair thanda hai. Naak bhi. Jacket abhi bhi aadha pehna tha, aur chadar neeche kone mein pad gayi thi.

“Ye kya hai...”

Weather app: Meguro: 9°C.

“Nau? Kal toh 15 tha. Kisne kiya ye?”

Phone ne kuch nahi bola. Phone ki bhi aadat hai.

Usne chadar shaal ki tarah lapeti aur parda hataya. Aasman bhoore se bhi zyada bhaari tha, jaise kisi ne upar se moti chadar daal di ho. Neeche vending machine ke paas ek aunty garam coffee ka dabba dono haathon mein pakde khadi thi, jaise koi khazana ho.

“Aaj ke din ka naam ‘Thand Saturday’ hai. Aur mai iska brand ambassador hu.”

Kitchen mein instant coffee ka dabba khaali nikla.

“Sach mein? Abhi? Is waqt?”

“Theek hai. Mai ek bahadur insaan hu. Mai thand se nahi darta.”

Usne darwaza khola, thandi hawa seedhe chehre pe lagi, aur usne darwaza wapas band kar diya. Do second baad phir khola, is baar jacket ke upar ek sweater pehen ke.

Seedhiyon pe padosan Tanaka-san jhaadu laga rahi thi. Wo Ryan ko hamesha “beta” bulati thi.

“Beta, aaj bahut thand hai na. Mere ghutne subah se bol rahe the. Maine apne pati se kaha tha ki baarish aane wali hai.”

“Baarish toh nahi lag rahi. Par kuch toh aa raha hai.”

“Kuch toh aa raha hai,” Tanaka-san ne sar hilaya. “Gale mein kuch pehno, beta.”

“Ji. Pehen liya hai.”

“Gale mein.”

“...Jaa raha hu.”

Konbini mein garam hawa ka jhonka laga, aur Ryan ne sukoon se saans li. Counter pe wahi college wala ladka, ab mote jacket mein, laal naak ke saath.

“Aap aaj subah subah?”

“Subah nahi, dopahar hai.”

“Mere liye subah hi hai. Dusra ladka nahi aaya, toh mai raat bhar yahin tha.”

“Tu insaan hai ya system?”

“Pata nahi. Mujhe neend nahi aa rahi.”

Ryan ne coffee ke teen packet, garam oden aur ek heat pack rakha counter pe.

“Aaj sabhi ye le rahe hain,” ladke ne kaha. “Subah se paanch heat pack bik gaye. Garam coffee aadhi se zyada khatam.”

“Sab ko ek saath thand lag gayi?”

“Ajeeb hai na?”

Bahar ek bachcha apni maa ka haath pakde jaa raha tha, uske munh se halki bhaap nikal rahi thi. “Haan,” Ryan ne dheere se kaha. “Ajeeb hai.”

Ghar aake Ryan ne heater on kiya aur coffee banayi. Phone buzz hua.

“Ye kya tha?”

Wo khidki pe gaya. Aasman bilkul pehle jaisa tha. Kuch nahi tha. Bahut upar, Meguro ke aasman mein baadal ab pehle se thode neeche aa gaye the.
